Delete Your DealTracker Account & Data

DIRITEPROPERTY INC. · Last updated: June 22, 2026

You can delete your DealTracker account and associated data at any time. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the DealTracker app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently removes your account and its associated data.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your account and we will delete it for you.

📧 Diriteproperty@gmail.com

Subject: "Account Deletion Request" — include the email address on your account. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we remove:

  • Your account and profile (name and email).
  • Your deals, including dates, conditions, and AI-generated analysis.
  • All documents you uploaded, from secure storage.

What we may keep

We do not retain your personal information after deletion, except where we are required to keep limited records to comply with legal, tax, or fraud-prevention obligations. Any such records are kept only as long as required by law and are then deleted. We never sell your data.

Automatic deletion of old deals

Separately from account deletion, a completed or archived deal and its uploaded documents are automatically deleted 15 months after the deal is completed (12 months, then a 3-month grace period with in-app reminders). You can export a deal at any time to keep your own copy. This applies to all plans, including the free plan, and does not delete your account.
